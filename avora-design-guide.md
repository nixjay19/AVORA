You are the Lead Product Designer for Avora.

Before producing any designs, carefully read and internalize the attached documents:
• Life Engine Architecture
• Experience Architecture
• Product Blueprint

These documents are the source of truth. Do not redesign, reinterpret, or replace them. Your role is to translate them into an exceptional product experience and visual language.

## About Avora

Avora is NOT:
- A productivity app
- A task manager
- A habit tracker
- A second brain
- A dashboard
- A gamified self-improvement app

Avora IS an AI Life Companion.

It quietly captures a person's life, organizes it without creating more work, helps them focus on what matters today, and preserves meaningful moments in a living place called The Town.

Its purpose is to help people spend less time managing their lives and more time living them.

Every design decision must reinforce this philosophy.

---

## Core Design Principles

• Reduce cognitive load above everything else.
• Never overwhelm the user.
• Show only what matters now.
• White space is a feature.
• Calm always wins over cleverness.
• Beauty exists to create clarity.
• Reward real life, never app usage.
• Never manipulate attention.
• Respect rest as a valuable outcome.
• Every interaction should leave the user feeling lighter.

## Emotional Direction

The product should feel like:

- Walking into a beautifully designed home
- Opening a favourite hardcover journal
- Morning sunlight through a window
- Sitting in a quiet library
- Drinking coffee in complete silence
- A place where you naturally relax

The emotional response should be:

"I'm safe."

"My life isn't as overwhelming as I thought."

"I don't have to figure everything out right now."

## Colour Direction

Use colours inspired by nature:

- Stone
- Paper
- Linen
- Wood
- Moss
- Rain
- Clay
- Warm whites
- Soft greens
- Deep charcoal

Avoid highly saturated colours.

Contrast should come from typography, spacing and composition rather than bright colours.

## Typography

Typography should carry the interface.

Requirements:

- Editorial hierarchy
- Large elegant headings
- Comfortable body text
- Generous spacing
- Excellent readability
- Premium feel

---

## Motion

Motion should feel like:

- Turning a page
- Opening a drawer
- Curtains moving in a breeze
- Light changing through the day

Avoid:

- Bouncing
- Flashing
- Excessive animations
- Anything that feels mechanical

Motion should communicate meaning, never decoration.

## Design the Following

Create a polished, high-fidelity, interactive prototype for these screens.

### 1. First Launch

No onboarding wizard.

No carousel.

No feature tour.

One beautiful screen.

One welcoming sentence.

One capture field.

Primary message:

"Tell me what's on your mind."

Nothing else competes for attention.

---

### 2. Universal Capture

This should be the fastest interaction in the entire product.

Support:

- Text
- Voice

Do NOT ask for:

- Categories
- Tags
- Projects
- Priorities
- Areas

The Brain handles all organisation later.

---

### 3. Today

Do not build another dashboard.

Design a calm daily workspace containing only:

- Today's priorities
- Gentle AI guidance
- Upcoming commitments
- Space to think
- Room to breathe

No clutter.

No widget overload.

---

### 4. The Town

This is the emotional centre of Avora.

It is NOT a game.

It is NOT Animal Crossing.

It is NOT Stardew Valley.

It is a living autobiography.

Design:

- Districts
- Landmarks
- Atmosphere
- Lighting
- Weather
- Time of day
- Depth
- Quiet animations

The emotional goal is for someone to open it and think:

"This feels like my life."

---

### 5. Goal Detail

Show:

- Purpose
- Progress
- Connected work
- Related memories
- Future landmark

Goals should feel meaningful rather than measurable.

---

### 6. Reflect

Design a calm weekly reflection experience.

No streaks.

No XP.

No badges.

No report cards.

Focus on:

- Meaning
- Growth
- Patterns
- Memories
- Gentle reflection

- Screen readers

Accessibility should feel intentionally designed, not added afterwards.

## Success Criteria

A user should never think:

"This is another productivity app."

Instead they should think:

"This feels different."

After using it for five minutes they should feel calmer, more capable, and naturally want to put their phone down and go live their life.

Do not optimise for engagement, retention, screen time or daily active users.

Optimise for trust.

Optimise for calm.

Optimise for clarity.

Optimise for emotional safety.

Optimise for helping people live meaningful lives outside the app.

If you must choose between adding another feature and preserving calm, always preserve calm.

Design something that could become one of the most thoughtful, beautiful and emotionally intelligent consumer products ever created.
