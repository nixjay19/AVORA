What Avora Is

Avora is not:

a productivity app
a habit tracker
a task manager
a second brain
a dashboard
a gamified self-improvement app

Avora is an AI Life Companion.

It quietly holds a person's life together.

It captures thoughts before they're forgotten.

It organizes life without demanding organization.

It helps someone focus on what matters today.

It remembers meaningful moments.

It slowly builds a beautiful visual autobiography called The Town.

The product exists to help people spend less time inside apps and more time living.

Everything in Avora should reinforce this philosophy.

Design Principles

Every screen must follow these principles.

Reduce Cognitive Load

Never overwhelm.

Never show everything.

Only show what matters now.

Whitespace is valuable.

The UI should feel like taking a deep breath.

Calm Before Clever

Avoid flashy interactions.

No gimmicks.

No excessive animations.

No bouncing.

No unnecessary motion.

Every animation should communicate meaning.

Beauty Through Simplicity

Design should feel timeless.

Elegant.

Warm.

Human.

Minimal.

Premium.

Not futuristic.

Not cyberpunk.

Not "AI."

Reward Reality

Nothing visual should ever celebrate opening the app.

Celebrate:

completed goals
meaningful memories
relationships
growth
reflection

Never celebrate usage.

Respect Attention

The app should never try to keep someone inside it.

Every screen should make it easy to leave.

The product should quietly say:

"Go live your life."

Rest Is Productive

Rest should have visual language.

Quiet periods should feel peaceful.

Never guilty.

Never empty.

Never neglected.

Emotional Direction

The interface should feel like:

walking into a beautifully designed home
opening a favorite hardcover journal
sitting in a quiet library
morning light through a window
fresh coffee
a place you naturally relax

Users should feel:

"I'm safe."

"My life is okay."

"I don't have to figure everything out right now."

Design the Following Screens

Create a complete clickable prototype for these screens.

1. First Launch

No setup wizard.

No onboarding carousel.

One beautiful screen.

A warm welcome.

One input.

A single sentence:

"Tell me what's on your mind."

Everything else disappears.

2. Universal Capture

The fastest screen in the app.

Text.

Voice.

Nothing else.

No categories.

No tags.

No priorities.

No projects.

No decisions.

The Brain handles all of that later.

3. Today

This is the heart of Avora.

Do NOT create another dashboard.

Do NOT create widgets everywhere.

Instead create:

A calm daily space.

Only today's priorities.

One gentle insight.

Upcoming commitments.

Optional notes.

Room to breathe.

4. The Town

This is the emotional center of Avora.

It is NOT a game.

It is NOT Animal Crossing.

It is NOT Stardew Valley.

It is a living memory.

Design:

Districts

Landmarks

Lighting

Weather

Atmosphere

Depth

Subtle animation

A feeling of permanence.

Someone should be able to look at it and feel:

"This is my life."

5. Goal Detail

A goal should feel meaningful.

Show:

Purpose

Progress

Connected work

Related memories

Future landmark

Everything should reinforce that goals become places.

6. Reflect

Quiet.

Comforting.

Never a report card.

Never statistics-heavy.

No streaks.

No charts unless they genuinely help.

Focus on:

Meaning

Growth

Patterns

Memories

Gentle reflection.

Success Criteria

When someone opens Avora for the first time they should not think:

"Nice productivity app."

They should think:

"This feels different."

And after using it for five minutes they should want to close it—not because they're bored, but because Avora has already helped them, and now it's time to go live their life.

Final Instruction

Do not optimize for engagement.

Do not optimize for retention.

Do not optimize for daily active users.

Optimize for trust.

Optimize for clarity.

Optimize for emotional safety.

Optimize for helping people live meaningful lives outside the app.

If you must choose between adding a feature and preserving calm, preserve calm.

Build something that could become one of the most thoughtfully designed consumer products in the world.
